A Ministry of Restoration and Hope

Founded in 2021 by Apostle Nathi Khumalo, For His Grace Ministries is a Christ-centred ministry in Ratanda. Our focus is restoration: helping people who feel lost or wounded find their way back to Christ and the hope found in Him.

We believe this ministry is sent by God to serve people in need and remind those who have forgotten that Jesus is still and always will be Christ. We seek to follow God's leading and serve our community through outreach and practical support for those in need.
